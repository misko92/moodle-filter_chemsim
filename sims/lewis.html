Electronegativities (Pauling, as in the notes): H 2.1, Be 1.5, B 2.0, C 2.5, N 3.0, O 3.5, F 4.0, Si 1.8, P 2.1, S 2.5, Cl 3.0, Br 2.8, I 2.5, Xe 2.6. A bond with ΔEN of 0.4 or less is treated as nonpolar.
Rules used: H gets 2 electrons; C, N, O and F get exactly 8; B and Be may have fewer than 8; atoms in period 3 and below may have more than 8 when they are the central atom. The best structure is the one with the smallest formal charges, with any negative formal charge on the most electronegative atom.
